Research Architecture & Policy Framework

Foundational principles, policy sources, and pipeline design.

1. Separation Principle

Detection ≠ Policy ≠ Action

The ML model evaluates audio transcripts for intrinsic toxicity signals. The policy engine maps those signals to platform-specific guidelines. The final human or platform decision remains autonomous.

2. Dual Platform Policies

One detected event is independently evaluated under:
• YouTube Community Guidelines (Hate Speech, Harassment, Violent Threats)
• Meta Community Standards (Standard 13 Hate Speech, Standard 14 Bullying & Harassment)
Timestamps remain identical.

3. Policy Adaptation

When policy rules or sensitivity thresholds change (e.g. from standard v1 to heightened civic v2), policy assessments shift dynamically without retraining the ML model.
